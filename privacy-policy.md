# Tab Shortcuts - Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

Tab Shortcuts does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.

The extension operates entirely locally in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no network requests.

## Permissions

- **tabs**: Duplicate and pin tabs
- **activeTab**: Interact with the current tab when you trigger a command
- **commands**: Register keyboard shortcuts
- **scripting**: Inject the prevent-close functionality into the active tab on demand
- **host_permissions (github.com)**: Collapse translation files and convert timestamps on GitHub pages

## Contact

For questions or concerns, open an issue at https://github.com/triuzzi/tab-shortcuts/issues
